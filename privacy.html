Privacy notice

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Operator: Sunny Patel.

1. Scope and contact

This notice describes the internal Project Clio application and its use of YouTube API Services. Contact clipfarmsap@gmail.com about access, privacy or deletion. The application currently has no public user-registration or subscription service.

2. Information processed

Depending on enabled features, Clio processes authorized channel IDs and names, OAuth credentials, video and source metadata, public comments and timestamp signals, channel/video statistics and authorized analytics, source or supplied media, audio, transcripts, captions, generated upload metadata, and job/delivery records. Full comment text can be handled temporarily during analysis; retention varies by processing stage.

3. Purposes and service providers

Information is used to discover candidates, select and edit highlights, create captions and metadata, upload to authorized channels, track processing and delivery, and show analytics to the operator. Explicitly enabled workflows may publish automatically without approval of each individual clip.

Clio uses YouTube API Services. Use of those services is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Configured Google Gemini features can receive source URLs, media excerpts with audio, transcripts, captions, prompts and generation requests. Provider-side processing and retention are governed by the applicable provider terms; a store:false request does not guarantee that no provider data is retained.

4. Storage and access

Production data and OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's private homelab. Credential files have restricted file permissions; production credentials are not embedded in this public documentation. Production access is limited to authorized users. No claim of encryption at rest or absolute security is made.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary capture and review footage is removed as workflows finish or fail. Deferred upload media may remain for a retry window of up to seven days. Copies deliberately exported to an operator-controlled computer or supplied through a media inbox remain under that operator's control. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until removed there; deleting a local copy does not delete the published video.

Operator-managed procedure: the operator will refresh or delete YouTube API data within applicable YouTube policy periods, ordinarily 30 calendar days unless YouTube expressly permits longer retention. Following revocation of authorization or a valid deletion request, associated API data will be deleted as soon as possible and within 30 calendar days. These checks and deletion requests may require manual action; this notice does not claim every deletion step is automated. Job records and cached analytics must be included where they contain associated API data.

To request deletion, email clipfarmsap@gmail.com and identify the relevant channel or data. The operator may verify authority before disclosing or deleting account-associated information. Removing an OAuth connection alone is not treated as proof that all associated cached data was deleted.

6. Revocation and workflow controls

The operator can pause automatic workflows, cancel eligible queued uploads, disconnect channels and remove cached data. Access can also be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access and triggers the associated-data deletion procedure above.

7. Changes

This notice will be updated if the application's users, purposes, providers or data practices change. Material changes must be reflected in this notice and in any necessary YouTube compliance review.
