Project quotas, channel-specific upload limits and rate limits remain in effect. Separate projects must not be rotated to artificially increase quota for the same use case.

The operator will follow the privacy notice, including its manual refresh, deletion and revocation procedures. Public documentation must not include production passwords, API keys, refresh tokens, upload-session URLs, home address, private footage or private channel analytics.

Captures, AI selections, processing and publication can fail or be delayed. Clio does not guarantee clip quality, virality, monetization or upload capacity. Contact clipfarmsap@gmail.com about operation or these internal rules.
